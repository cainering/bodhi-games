Built for Bodhi

For parents

Brain Breaks are short learning pauses instead of ads. At the end of every level, whether Bodhi finishes it or starts it over, he answers a few questions. Questions get a little harder as the game level goes up. Levels are saved on this iPad. Players can switch on the break screen.

The 💬 bubble lets Bodhi chat with Tommy. It asks for the family code once on this iPad. Stars can be earned in Practice and spent on hints. A new game costs 20 stars.

Treasure Map finds a level for the subjects that are turned on. Those levels are saved for Brain Breaks. He can quit and come back, or retake a subject anytime.

Book Club is for a book he finished. He picks the book, then tells Tommy about it in the chat. A long book can earn 20 stars. A short book does not.

Homework is a photo of one worksheet. The check can take about a minute. It can award stars, up to 10 for a sheet and 20 homework stars a day. Hints never include the answer.
